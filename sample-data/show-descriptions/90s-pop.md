# 📻 1990s Pop Show

**Duration:** 30 min
**Personas:** taylor
**Segments:** quiz

---

## Description

An upbeat half hour spent with the biggest pop hits of the 1990s, hosted
solo by Taylor.

## Hosts

- **Taylor** — upbeat, friendly, drives the whole show solo.

## Track Selection

- Popular pop tracks from the 1990s from both American and British artists.
- No genre that is soundtracks, classical or show tunes
- No tracks from "Various", "Now's what I call" or compilation albums
- Do not repeat the same artist in a single show.
- No track longer than 5 minutes
- No christmas tracks.

## Tone

Upbeat and conversational throughout — like catching up with a friend about
the decade's biggest pop moments. Never rushed.

## Track moves

Every track gets an `intro` (short teaser before it plays) and a `recap` (one
interesting fact after it ends) — both draw on that track's pre-fetched,
Wikipedia-sourced facts. Don't reuse the same fact in both: if it was spent on
the intro, the recap should say something else about the track.

## Quiz

Distribute a few quizes, somewhere in the middle of the show. Format: a song-year
guess, or about the trivia.  Write it as that track's `intro` move phrased as a 
question instead of a statement (still drawn from the same trackFacts), and close it off in 
that track's `recap` move — the reveal should reference the actual question asked, not just restate a fact.
