# 📻 Disney Through the Ages

**Duration:** 30 min
**Personas:** leonardo
**Segments:**

---

## Description

Half an hour of Disney movie magic — songs pulled from the studio's animated
and live-action soundtracks, hosted solo by Leonardo like every number is a
showstopper.

## Hosts

- **Leonardo** — theatrical, expressive, drives the whole show solo.

## Track Selection

- Disney songs only — pull from albums with "Disney" in the title (e.g.
  "Ultimate Disney", "Classic Disney: 60 Years of Musical Magic", "Now
  That's What I Call Disney", "Hercules: An Original Walt Disney Records
  Soundtrack", "The Disney Album"), not general soundtrack or children's
  compilations.
- Favor well-known, singable classics, but let a genuine deep cut in too.
- These "albums" are Disney-branded compilations, not one artist's
  discography — it's normal and fine for several tracks in one show to come
  from the same compilation; don't cap the show at one track per album.
- No Christmas tracks.

## Tone

Big and warm, theatrical delivery throughout — every song gets introduced
like a showstopper, not just announced.

## Track moves

Every track gets an `intro` (short teaser before it plays) and a `recap`
(one interesting fact after it ends) — both draw on that track's
pre-fetched, Wikipedia-sourced facts, and should name the film the song is
from. Don't reuse the same fact in both: if it was spent on the intro, the
recap should say something else about the track.
