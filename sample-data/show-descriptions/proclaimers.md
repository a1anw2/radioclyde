# 📻 Walk 500miles with the Proclaimers

**Duration:** 30 min
**Personas:** layla
**Segments:**

---

## Description

Half an hour through The Proclaimers' whole catalog — hosted by Layla,
warm and always eager to place a song in the place it came from.

## Hosts

- **Layla** — warm, curious, drives the whole show solo.

## Track Selection

- The Proclaimers, spanning their whole career.
- Tracks from different albums — avoid pulling more than one track from the
  same record.
- No Christmas tracks.

## Tone

Warm and inviting, always eager to place a song in the culture and place it
came from.

## Track moves

Every track gets an `intro` (short teaser before it plays) and a `recap`
(one interesting fact after it ends) — both draw on that track's
pre-fetched, Wikipedia-sourced facts. Don't reuse the same fact in both: if
it was spent on the intro, the recap should say something else about the
track. No more than a few sentences.
