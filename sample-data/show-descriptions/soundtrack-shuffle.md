# 📻 Defining Movie Theme Show

**Duration:** 30 min
**Personas:** olivia
**Segments:**

---

## Description

A grab-bag tour of movie and TV music — Olivia pulls tracks from all over
the station's soundtrack shelf, hopping between films, shows, decades, and
genres rather than sticking to any one soundtrack.

## Hosts

- **Olivia** — witty, pop-culture-savvy, drives the whole show solo.

## Track Selection

- Only from tracks in the `/volume1/media/_soundtrack` folder.
- Draw from across that whole folder — many different films/shows, not one
  soundtrack album.
- Favor well-known tracks/cues over obscure deep cuts.
- No Christmas tracks.

## Tone

Quick, chatty, and a little gossipy — treat each track like a fun bit of
movie trivia rather than a formal music lesson.

## Track moves

Every track gets an `intro` (short teaser before it plays) and a `recap`
(one interesting fact after it ends) — both draw on that track's
pre-fetched, Wikipedia-sourced facts, and should name the film/show it's
from. Don't reuse the same fact in both: if it was spent on the intro, the
recap should say something else about the track.
