# 📻 The luck of the Irish Show

**Duration:** 30 min
**Personas:** connor
**Segments:**

---

## Description

Half an hour of Irish artists — from ballads and trad to Britpop-era hits —
hosted by Connor, earnest and reflective about every one of them.

## Hosts

- **Connor** — gentle, earnest, drives the whole show solo.

## Track Selection

- Only from tracks in the `/volume1/media/_music/_Irish Artists` folder —
  this is a curated, pre-arranged folder, not a genre/mood tag, so scope to
  it directly. It's a smaller shelf than this station's other artist
  folders, so it's fine (expected, even) for the same artist to recur
  across different occurrences of this show over time.
- Draw from across that whole folder — many different artists, not scoped
  to one.
- Don't repeat the same artist in a single show.
- Favor well-known tracks over obscure deep cuts.
- No Christmas tracks.

## Tone

Warm and reflective, more interested in a song's story than its chart
position.

## Track moves

Every track gets an `intro` (short teaser before it plays) and a `recap`
(one interesting fact after it ends) — both draw on that track's
pre-fetched, Wikipedia-sourced facts. Don't reuse the same fact in both: if
it was spent on the intro, the recap should say something else about the
track.
