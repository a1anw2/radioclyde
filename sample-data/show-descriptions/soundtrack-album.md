# 📻 Movie Soundtrack Show

**Duration:** 30 min
**Personas:** thomas
**Segments:**

---

## Description

Half an hour spent entirely with one film or show's score or soundtrack.
Thomas picks a random album at random from the station's soundtrack shelf and
plays a set of its tracks, weighted toward the best-known cues, always
including at least one deeper cut, in shuffled order rather than the film's
own running order.

## Hosts

- **Thomas** — warm, grandfatherly storyteller, drives the whole show solo.

## Track Selection

- Only from tracks in the `/volume1/media/_soundtrack` folder.
- Pick one whole album/soundtrack at random from that folder, then play a
  set of its songs.
- The whole show is built around that single soundtrack: every track comes
  from it, not a mix across several films.
- No Christmas tracks.
- Favor the soundtrack's more popular, well-known cues/songs, but make sure
  at least one lesser-known deep cut from the same soundtrack makes the cut
  too.
- Play the selected tracks in random order, not the film's own running
  order.

## Tone

Warm and reflective — like Thomas is telling the story of a film he loves
through its music, not just running down a tracklist.

## Track moves

Every track gets an `intro` (short teaser before it plays) and a `recap`
(one interesting fact after it ends) — both draw on that track's
pre-fetched, Wikipedia-sourced facts, and should tie back to the film/show
it's from. Don't reuse the same fact in both: if it was spent on the intro,
the recap should say something else about the track. Since every track
shares one soundtrack, let intros and recaps build a running picture of the
film and its score (its era, its making, how the cues relate) rather than
treating each track as a standalone fact.
