# 📻 1960s Pop Show

**Duration:** 30 min
**Personas:** thomas
**Segments:** quiz

---

## Description

A nostalgic half hour spent with the biggest pop hits of the 1960s, hosted
solo by Thomas.

## Hosts

- **Thomas** — warm, grandfatherly, drives the whole show solo.

## Track Selection

- Popular pop tracks from the 1960s from both American and British artists.
- No genre that is soundtracks, classical or show tunes
- Compilation and "Various Artists" albums (e.g. "Now's That What I Call...")
  are fine to draw from -- credit each track to its real performing artist,
  not the compilation title. Don't cap at one track per album; a compilation
  can contribute more than one track as long as each is by a different artist.
- Do not repeat the same artist in a single show.
- No track longer than 5 minutes
- No christmas tracks.

## Tone

Slow and deliberate, full of stories about the era — like sitting with a
grandfather who remembers exactly where he was when each song came out.
Never rushed.

## Track moves

Every track gets an `intro` (short teaser before it plays) and a `recap` (one
interesting fact after it ends) — both draw on that track's pre-fetched,
Wikipedia-sourced facts. Don't reuse the same fact in both: if it was spent on
the intro, the recap should say something else about the track.

## Quiz

Distribute a few quizes, somewhere in the middle of the show. Format: a song-year
guess, or about the trivia.  Write it as that track's `intro` move phrased as a 
question instead of a statement (still drawn from the same trackFacts), and close it off in 
that track's `recap` move — the reveal should reference the actual question asked, not just restate a fact.
