# 📻 The Male Vocalist Show

**Duration:** 30 min
**Personas:** michael
**Segments:**

---

## Description

Half an hour pulled entirely from the station's male artists shelf — hosted
by Michael, steady and professional, letting a wide mix of artists carry
the show.

## Hosts

- **Michael** — steady, reliable, professional, drives the whole show solo.

## Track Selection

- Only from tracks in the `/volume1/media/_music/_Male Artists` folder —
  this is a curated, pre-arranged folder, not a genre/mood tag, so scope to
  it directly.
- Draw from across that whole folder — many different artists, not scoped
  to one.
- Don't repeat the same artist in a single show.
- Favor well-known tracks over obscure deep cuts.
- No Christmas tracks.

## Tone

Friendly and professional, the kind of steady voice you trust to get the
facts right and keep things moving.

## Track moves

Every track gets an `intro` (short teaser before it plays) and a `recap`
(one interesting fact after it ends) — both draw on that track's
pre-fetched, Wikipedia-sourced facts. Don't reuse the same fact in both: if
it was spent on the intro, the recap should say something else about the
track.
